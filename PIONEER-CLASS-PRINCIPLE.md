## 1. Purpose

This document records the pioneering, cloud-native, agentic-AI standard that the
ThreadForge suite intends to uphold. It is written as the first canonical
"prompt-level" (founding) statement for the project: the authored vision the
suite measures itself against as it advances from pilot readiness to public,
industrial-scale deployment.

It does not replace evidence. Every marketing claim, capability declaration, and
release status elsewhere in this repository must still be backed by the
evidence and pricing documents cited in `threadforge/website/public` and the
product notices (`PRODUCT-NOTICES-DRAFT.md`).

## 2. Core principle (the "Class Pioneer")

1. **Consumer-first sovereignty.** The suite serves the consumer/operator before it
   serves any platform. Ownership of data, decisions, and outcomes stays with the
   operator unless explicitly delegated.
2. **Agentic intelligence with human oversight.** AI agents may draft, route, audit,
   and propose; they may never merge, ship, or commit without human approval. The
   agent stack proposes; the human disposes.
3. **Open, auditable, protected.** Intellectual rights are reserved by authorship
   and publication; the work must stand stably on its own as a contribution, not
   as a shadow or derivative service. Processes, forms, evidence, and reviews are
   opened for qualification and publication rather than obscured.
4. **Protected contribution over fabricated authority.** Commercial, legal, or
   licensing claims are made only where supported (see `LICENSING.md`, `EULA-DRAFT-*.md`,
   `COMMERCIAL-TERMS-DRAFT-*.md`, `TRADEMARK-CLEARANCE-*.md`). No claim in this
   document overrides the evidence and notice discipline of the product.
5. **Evolving, universal cloud posture.** Cloud, IoT, iOS, Microsoft/industrial
   integration, and live AI stacks are treated as evolving layers with safe
   protocols and healthy isolation, moving with human intellect and machine
   intelligence together — not as a fixed, one-time state.
6. **Trigonometric / infinite-cell accounting.** Where calculation or
   harmonisation is required, the accounting and reconciliation model must
   tolerate continuous evolution: algorithms drive the protocol, and the protocol
   evolves in chain. No single static constant may be treated as final truth.
7. **Contribution without erasure.** The authored suit itself must remain
   standalone and steadfast while ongoing evolution happens alongside it. New
   capability layers do not nullify the standing core.

## 3. What this means for releases

- New capability is added in layers (`CAPABILITIES-*.md`, `RELEASE-*.md`) with
  evidence and a review trail.
- Cloud/IoT/AI integration is declared only where isolated, safe, and
  reversible.
- Pricing, claims, and status text follow the owner-approved evidence list
  (`PRICING-*.md`) and product notices; competitor prices are re-checked before
  each update.
- Every number or status in public material matches the latest recorded evidence.
