Your records stay on your own computer. Not even the next country.

Why it fits this market

Built local-first, not adapted for it.

Your own country, your own hardware

Windows PC, Android phone, or your own server with Docker: ThreadForge starts its own local service and keeps records in a workspace you control. There is no requirement to use any cloud, in any country. If you host it yourself, you choose the datacentre and the jurisdiction.

No sign-up, no analytics, no telemetry

The desktop and Android apps do not phone home. The website sets no cookies and runs no tracking. We are not describing a privacy policy here; it is a description of what the code does, the same claim made on every page of this site.

EU and UK screening, already built in

Supplier due diligence screens the official restricted-party lists you enable, covering US, EU and UK sources, and looks up the legal-entity registry. Every finding shows where it came from and what could not be checked. See the full detail on the institutions page.

German, if you want it

The product interface is English. The marketing site is also published in German at threadforgeofficial.com/de/, machine-drafted from the English source and marked as such — English is authoritative where the two differ. Product and tier names (ThreadForge, Essentials, Professional, Industrial, Custom) stay in English everywhere.

Pricing, currency and VAT

Stated plainly, not glossed over.

What is true today

  • Every price (Essentials $49, Professional $149, Industrial $299, Industrial Park $499, or Custom from $34) is in US dollars and excludes tax. This is stated once, here and on the pricing page, not hidden in a footnote.
  • An order is placed by emailing us, and we send a secure card payment link. There is no in-page checkout yet, so there is nothing that silently converts currency or adds VAT without you seeing it.
  • A licence is a one-time purchase per tier; an upgrade costs only the difference. There is no recurring charge to account for in a budget.

What we do not do yet

  • Quote or bill in euros or Swiss francs. We state the US dollar price; your bank or card network applies its own conversion.
  • Collect, remit or advise on VAT, Mehrwertsteuer or Swiss MWST. That is for your own accountant, same as any US-dollar purchase from outside the EU or Switzerland.
  • Issue an EU-format VAT invoice automatically. Ask when you order; we will tell you plainly whether we can provide what you need.
