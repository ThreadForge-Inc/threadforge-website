Three kinds of institution

The same suite, used three ways.

Every tier has the same rule: nothing is sent, merged or repaired until a person approves it. Teaching labs, ministries and boardrooms all care about that.

Academic and research

Business, engineering, supply-chain and computer-science departments get a working tool, not slides. Students compare sourcing origins on landed cost, build capital-project cost plans with PERT schedules, screen suppliers against the official US, EU and UK lists with a source for every finding, and watch an engineering agent open a draft pull request only after sealed tests and an independent review pass.

The browser demo runs on sample data for an invented company, so a class can use it without accounts or personal data. Explore the ThreadForge Online Academy & PWI .NET Practice Lab for 16 chronological research monographs, 4-tier free courses (Apprentice to Titan), and 5 interactive practice labs.

Public institutions and NGOs

A one-time price means no recurring budget line to defend every year. Records stay on your own PC or server, the suite works offline, and an AI model can run on your own hardware, so citizen, beneficiary and donor records do not need to leave your premises.

Separate organizations, revocable roles (admin, operator, finance, viewer), an audit trail, consent-aware campaigns and exact-cent ledgers support the oversight these bodies answer to.

Commercial corporations

Multi-company groups, manufacturers and trading houses run stock, orders, trade sourcing, supplier due diligence, capital projects and HRBP coordination in one login, for every subsidiary, with unlimited seats.

Industrial Park includes the source code for your version, so your own engineers can audit it, extend it and build internal binaries, with no dependency on Threadforge Inc. to keep running.

Incubators, chambers and trade bodies

Programmes that support many small companies can point members to one tool for stock, orders, landed cost and supplier screening, at a one-time price an early-stage business can afford.

Each member company buys and keeps its own licence and its own records. ThreadForge does not pool member data anywhere.

What we never claim

  • Security or compliance certifications we have not received, such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001.
  • That a due-diligence report is a sanctions clearance or legal advice.
  • Antivirus detection rates. Detection is Microsoft Defender’s.
  • Benchmark wins or “best in class” rankings.
