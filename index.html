The ThreadForge principle

Nothing changes until you say yes.

Three very different jobs follow one rule. ThreadForge does the checking, drafting and preparing. The decision that changes something stays with you.

Your customers

Campaigns reach only contacts with recorded consent, and only after you approve the revision. Each message is captured for a final read before any delivery service is switched on.

approve revision → capture → send

Your code

The agent opens a draft pull request once tests and an independent review pass. It has no way to merge. Your main branch moves only when you merge it.

test → review → draft PR → you merge

Your computer

Diagnosis only reads. Every repair waits for an explicit yes, offers a restore point before system changes, and is written to a journal you can open.

diagnose → you approve → repair → journal

Dependencies that hold

An item cannot reach Done while something it depends on is still open, and ThreadForge refuses a dependency that would make a loop.

Limits on work in progress

Set how many items each column may hold. A full column says so and refuses the next card until one moves on.

Supplier due diligence, added in 10.5

Ask about a supplier. Get checks you can trace.

Write the check you need the way you would ask a colleague: the supplier, its country and the product. ThreadForge screens official sanctions and restricted-party lists, looks up the legal entity, compares national export statistics and analyses shipment records from your own customs-data subscription. One report shows what it found, where each finding came from and what it could not check.

Five checks in one report

Restricted parties
The US Consolidated Screening List and the EU and UK sanctions lists, held in your workspace. The supplier, its parent companies and its buyers are all screened.
Legal entity
The supplier’s LEI record from GLEIF: legal name, status, registered address and reported parents
Shipment records
Shipments, buyers, HS codes and declared origins from your own licensed export or API, for example from 52wmb, ImportGenius or Panjiva
Trade statistics
The supplier country’s recorded exports of the product over three years, from UN Comtrade
Your records
Earlier purchase orders, supplier contacts and previous reports in your organization

Write it in plain words

Example request
Due diligence on Acme Lighting Co., Ltd. in Viet Nam before we issue a purchase order for LED panels (HS 9405.11) shipped to the USA, like 52wmb.com

ThreadForge shows how it read the request before it runs, and every report ends with one of four recommendations:

Hold
A problem to resolve first, such as a strong listed match or an inactive legal entity
Review
Warnings for a person to check before ordering
No issues found
Nothing flagged in the sources checked; your usual procurement steps still apply
Incomplete
Screening did not run, so the report cannot support a purchase decision yet

Official sources, off until you choose

The workspace owner enables each source. Only five fixed government and registry hosts can be contacted, and list downloads send no customer data.

Evidence with every finding

Each match shows the list, programme, listed countries and match score, plus when the list was downloaded and its SHA-256 digest.

Your licensed data stays yours

Services named in a request are recognised, never scraped or copied. Import your own subscription’s export, or connect its licensed API.

What due diligence won’t do. A report supports a person’s decision. It isn’t legal advice, a sanctions clearance or a credit report. Name screening can miss renamed parties and flag unrelated ones with similar names. Shipment analysis is only as complete as your licensed data.

App builder, new in 10.6

Name an app you like. Get an original one, built and tested.

Describe the website, app or game you want and the product it should be like: a music player like Spotify, an endless runner like Subway Surfers or a film catalog like MovieBox. Say what should be better. A team of agents researches what people expect and writes a brief. After you approve it, the agents write, review and test the code and open a draft pull request.

Understands and checks rights

Finds the references, the kind of product, the platform and where the content comes from, and shows them before anything runs. The new product gets its own name, art and code.

Researches and writes a brief

A research agent uses the model you connect, and any MCP tools you allow, to plan features, improvements, screens and acceptance tests.

Waits for your approval

You read the brief and choose a repository and a new folder. Nothing is built until the workspace owner approves it.

Builds, reviews and tests

A builder agent writes the app and a reviewer agent checks it, on a different model if you choose. An isolated runner runs the tests before a draft pull request opens.

What it builds

  • Websites, web apps and installable web apps for Android, iOS and desktop
  • HTML5 games with keyboard, touch and swipe controls and original art drawn in code
  • Streaming, store, booking, social and productivity apps with original placeholder content, ready for content you own or license
  • A tested project with a README, the rights notes and acceptance tests that run with node --test

What it won’t do

  • Copy another product’s name, logo, characters, artwork, sounds or code
  • Visit, scrape, download or decompile the reference, including APK files
  • Stream or link films, music or shows from other services, or get around DRM, paywalls or ads
  • Build login pages that collect other people’s passwords, malware or game cheats
  • Produce native store builds (APK, AAB or IPA) or publish to app stores

Your model, your repository, your approval. Research and builds use the AI model you connect, so their quality depends on that model: a free local model suits small apps and previews, and a larger provider model suits full builds. Every build is reviewed and tested before its draft pull request opens, and ThreadForge never merges.

Engineering agent

Describe the change. Review a tested draft.

Give ThreadForge a task in plain language. It reads the source, makes the change, runs your tests in a sealed container, has a second agent review the work and opens a draft pull request on GitHub. You stay the one who merges.

  1. Describe

    Write the task in a thread. Steer it while it runs.

  2. Inspect

    The builder reads the files it needs, by line range when they are large.

  3. Change

    Edits land on a new branch, inside file and patch-size limits.

  4. Test

    Your tests run in a disposable container with networking off.

  5. Review

    A separate reviewer reads the change and re-runs tests. It cannot edit.

  6. Draft PR

    Only a final passing run on unchanged source opens the pull request.

Guardrails built in

  • Sealed tests. Non-root containers with no network, a read-only source and root filesystem, and memory, CPU, process and time limits.
  • Credentials stay out. Model and GitHub keys never enter test containers. Agents cannot install packages or run your code on the host.
  • Publication gates. A final passing test run, non-empty test evidence, an unchanged source digest and a current base commit are all required.
  • Bounded runs. Step, token and time limits, cancellation and stale-worker detection keep every task in check.
  • Parallel threads. Run separate tasks at once. One active task per thread prevents conflicting writes.
  • Your model account. Connect an approved model provider. Usage is billed by your provider, inside your own budget.

PC care, added in 10.4

Say what’s wrong. Approve what gets fixed.

Describe a Windows problem in your own words. ThreadForge checks the computer without changing anything, explains what it found and offers numbered repairs. Each one runs only after you say yes, using tools Windows already includes.

Speed and startup
Uptime, free space on the system drive, memory use and the largest programs, startup entries
Malware and antivirus
Microsoft Defender status, signature age, active detections and flagged startup entries
Devices and drivers
Devices with Windows problem codes, their hardware IDs and pending restarts
Internet and network
Name resolution and Windows’ own connectivity test
Crashes and errors
App crashes, hangs and unexpected shutdowns in the last 14 days
Windows Update
Pending restarts and update service state
System files
Component store health checked with DISM

Read-only first

Diagnosis writes no files. Commands are fixed, with no shell and no text written by a model. A run without your yes changes nothing.

A journal of every repair

Each approved run, including one refused for missing admin rights, is written to your profile so you can see exactly what happened.

No extra engine or drivers

Detection and removal belong to Microsoft Defender. ThreadForge adds no scanning engine, kernel component, background service or driver.

What PC care won’t do. It can’t fix failing hardware, recover deleted files or guarantee that a Windows tool repairs every fault. It never downloads drivers from third-party sites. It tells you what it ran and what happened.

Yes. The live demo asks for an email first. A work address and a personal address both work, and a company domain is not required. The address stays in that browser and is not sent to us. Each browser then gets one free 60-minute session of the whole suite with sample data, including the TradeMath calculators. Business workflows work for real; AI, PC and data-source results are simulated and labelled. After that, buy it once, from $49, and run it on your own PC or server with your own data.

        

        

          Who is ThreadForge for?
          
Small teams that want stock, orders, finance records and campaigns in one private place, and owners or developers who want careful help with their code and their Windows PCs.

        

        

          Do I need an AI subscription?
          
No. The business workspace and PC care’s standard diagnosis use no AI model at all, and ThreadForge’s calculators do their own arithmetic. For AI help, a free local model on your own computer is the default: no account and no per-token bill. In our benchmark a free 7B coding model fixed 5 of 8 small engineering tasks on an ordinary computer, so for larger engineering work you can connect an OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini account or another OpenAI-compatible service. That provider bills you directly; ThreadForge adds no markup and sells no credits.

        

        

          Where does my data live?
          
On your PC or on a server you control. Each organization gets its own database. When you run an engineering task, the source files selected for it are sent to your configured model provider, so review that provider’s data policy first.

        

        

          Can ThreadForge clone an app, website or game?
          
It builds an original product inspired by the one you name: the same kind of features and flows, with its own name, artwork, text and code. It never copies, scrapes, downloads or decompiles the reference. A streaming app plays content you own, license or that is in the public domain, never streams from other services. ThreadForge won’t build login pages that collect passwords, malware or game cheats. The app builder is new in 10.6.

        

        

          What does ThreadForge cost?
          
Essentials is $49, Professional is $149, Industrial is $299 and Industrial Park is $499, each paid once. Only Industrial Park includes the source code, for use within your own organizations. Every tier has unlimited organizations and seats. There is no monthly, annual or per-user fee, and nothing is charged after you buy. Fixes for the version you bought are free; a later major version is a separate purchase that you choose. AI usage on a provider account, if you connect one, is billed by that provider. See pricing.

        

        

          Can ThreadForge replace 52wmb or ImportGenius?
          
No. Those services license shipment-level customs records, and ThreadForge doesn’t copy or scrape them. If you subscribe to one, import its export or connect its licensed API. ThreadForge then combines those records with official sanctions lists, the LEI registry and UN trade statistics in one report.

        

        

          Is a due-diligence report a sanctions clearance?
          
No. It is decision support for the person approving a supplier. It shows every finding with its source so that person can confirm it, and it lists what was not checked. Get legal advice for sanctions or export-control decisions.

        

        

          Is PC care an antivirus?
          
No. It works with Microsoft Defender, or with the antivirus product Windows reports. ThreadForge ships no scanning engine, driver or background service, and it doesn’t claim any detection rate.

        

        

          Can the engineering agent change my main branch?
          
No. It proposes draft pull requests after its own tests and an independent review pass. Merging is always your decision, made in GitHub.

        

        

          Is the Windows installer signed?
          
Not yet. Trusted code signing is in progress. Current builds are development builds, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning. Every build comes with SHA-256 checksums you can verify before you install.
