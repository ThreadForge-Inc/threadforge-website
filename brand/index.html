The name

The product is ThreadForge: one word, capital T and capital F. The company is Threadforge Inc., with a lowercase f, as registered by its owner.

Write it like this

  • ThreadForge keeps stock and orders together.
  • ThreadForge PC care
  • Threadforge Inc. (the company, in legal lines only)

Not like this

  • Thread Forge, Threadforge or THREADFORGE for the product
  • TF as a stand-alone name in public copy
  • A ® or ™ symbol. No trademark registration has been filed yet.

Logo

The mark shows three threads joining into one direction. The lime strand is the approved path. The wordmark is set in Gloock and supplied as outlines, so it looks right without the font installed.

Do

  • Leave clear space around the logo equal to half the mark’s height.
  • Keep the mark at least 16 px tall on screen and the full logo at least 120 px wide.
  • Use the reversed logo on forge green or any dark photo area.
  • Use the one-color mark where only one ink is available.

Don’t

  • Recolor the strands, stretch the logo or set the wordmark in another font.
  • Add shadows, outlines, gradients or animation to the mark.
  • Place the primary logo on a busy image or a mid-tone background.
  • Combine the mark with another company’s logo into a single lockup.

Also available: wordmark only (SVG), 512 px app icon (PNG) and social share image (PNG, 1200 × 630).

One sentence

ThreadForge is one private workspace for independent business operations. The same suite runs the work at one desk or across a larger industrial or corporate operation, at the published prices, and nothing changes until you approve it.

Short description, about 50 words

ThreadForge is one private workspace for independent business operations. The same suite keeps inventory, purchasing, sales, trade, manual finance and consent-aware campaigns connected, from one desk to a larger industrial or corporate operation. An engineering agent turns a request into a tested draft pull request, and PC care runs only the Windows repairs you approve. You pay the published price. It runs on your PC or your own server.

About the company

Threadforge Inc. builds ThreadForge, a private workspace for independent business operations. Version 10.17 is sold as a one-time purchase, from $49, with a 14-day Essentials trial. Learn more at threadforgeofficial.com.

Proof points you can use

  • Business records work without an AI subscription.
  • The engineering agent never merges. It opens draft pull requests after tests and an independent review pass.
  • PC care diagnosis is read-only, and every repair needs an explicit yes.
  • Version 10.17.2: 543 backend tests and all 52 required native Windows checks passed, and every download is checked against the build that made it.

Claims to leave out

  • Antivirus detection rates, lab certifications or “removes all malware”.
  • “Signed”, “certified”, “audited” or “enterprise-ready” until that evidence exists.
  • Benchmarks, rankings and superlatives such as “best ever”.
  • Prices, customer names or testimonials that haven’t been approved for publication.
