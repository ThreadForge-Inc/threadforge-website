Website privacy

Nothing you type on this site is sent to us.

This notice covers threadforgeofficial.com. Last updated October 22, 2026.

No cookies, analytics or trackers

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking code. It loads no third-party scripts, fonts, videos or embeds. Every file comes from this domain.

Your theme choice

If you pick a light or dark theme, your browser remembers that choice in its own local storage. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

TradeMath

The TradeMath desk calculates from the numbers you type. Those numbers and the result stay on the page in your browser. They are not sent to ThreadForge. If you download a result, your browser saves the file on your device.

The live demo

The live demo opens only after you enter an email address. A work address and a personal address are both accepted. A company domain is not required. The address, the sample records, your progress and the session’s start and end times stay in this browser’s local storage. They are not sent to ThreadForge or to anyone else, and this site does not email you. When the session ends, the sample records are deleted. The session times, the missions you finished and the email you entered remain, so this browser doesn’t start a second session. Clearing your browser data for this site removes those too.

The order form

The form doesn’t send or store anything. It prepares an email in your own mail app, which you can edit or discard. If you send it, we receive what you wrote and your email address, and we use them only to reply about your order.

Hosting

Like any website host, our hosting provider may record technical details such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, under its own privacy terms. We don’t receive or combine those records with anything else.

The ThreadForge software

This notice covers the website. The ThreadForge app for Windows and Android has its own app privacy policy.
