App privacy policy

Your workspace stays on your device.

This policy covers the ThreadForge app for Windows (Microsoft Store edition and direct download) and for Android, published by Threadforge Inc. Last updated October 31, 2026.

In short: ThreadForge keeps your workspace on your device. It has no sign-up, advertising, analytics or telemetry, and it sends nothing to Threadforge Inc.

What the app stores on your device

ThreadForge stores the records you enter in a workspace in the app's data folder on your device. These include products, stock movements, orders, invoices, payment records, customers and suppliers (which can include names, email addresses, phone numbers and addresses), campaign briefs, trade calculations and documents. They also include organizations, access tokens and an audit trail. The app's local service listens only on 127.0.0.1, so other computers cannot reach it. Threadforge Inc. cannot see this data.

On Android, the app asks for one permission, Internet access. It uses it for its own local service at 127.0.0.1 and for the features below, only when you turn them on.

What leaves your device, and only when you turn it on

ThreadForge contacts outside services only for features you enable or configure, and sends only what that feature needs:

  • Supplier due diligence: when the workspace owner enables official sources, the app downloads official lists from the United States (data.trade.gov), the European Union (webgate.ec.europa.eu) and the United Kingdom (sanctionslist.fcdo.gov.uk). It sends the supplier name you check to GLEIF (api.gleif.org), and HS codes and countries to UN Comtrade (comtradeapi.un.org).
  • AI models: if you connect an AI provider such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or another compatible service, the requests you make with AI features are sent to that provider under its own terms, with the content they need. A local model, such as Ollama on your PC, keeps those requests on your device.
  • GitHub: if you connect a GitHub account for engineering tasks or the app builder, the app reads and writes only the repositories you allow.
  • Email and payments: if you configure your own email (SMTP) server or Stripe test credentials, messages you approve and test checkouts are sent through them.
  • MCP servers and remote workspaces that you configure.
  • PC care: the diagnosis reads information on this PC, such as running programs, startup entries, Microsoft Defender status, device problems, disk space and memory. Its network check contacts Microsoft's connectivity test address (www.msftconnecttest.com). Crash reports are saved as a file on your PC and leave it only if you send them.

Credentials for these services stay in your workspace on your device.

What Threadforge Inc. receives

Nothing from the app. If you email support@threadforgeofficial.com, we receive what you send and use it only to reply.

Your choices

  • Export your records as CSV, and edit or delete them in the app.
  • Uninstalling the Microsoft Store or Android app deletes its data.
  • The direct-download Windows installer keeps your workspace folder (%APPDATA%\methodology-threadforge-desktop) so a reinstall can reuse it. You can delete that folder yourself.
  • The Android app does not take part in Android backup or device transfer.

Children

ThreadForge is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Access tokens stay in the app's private data folder, and the local service accepts connections only from this device. Keep your device, and your accounts with any services you connect, secure.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy with a new date.
