Honest boundaries

What this desk is, and isn't.

What it does

  • Calculates CBM, chargeable weight, landed cost and container loading from the figures you type in.
  • Compares an existing supplier against other origins on full landed cost, then stress-tests the result against a currency or freight shock you choose.
  • Scores a supplier and its origin's risk from the evidence you hold.
  • The same formulas as the ThreadForge Trade desk, running in your browser. Nothing you type here is uploaded.

What it doesn't do

  • Look up a live tariff, freight quote, or exchange rate. Duty, freight and rates are figures you enter, with their source and date.
  • Issue a customs assessment, a tax invoice, or legal or financial advice. These are estimates for planning.
  • Rank countries, banks or suppliers on your behalf.
