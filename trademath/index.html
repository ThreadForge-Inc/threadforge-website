Trade, procurement and project math, from figures you type.

Shipment math: carton volume, chargeable weight, landed cost, margins, containers, suppliers and a proforma check. International procurement: compare origins with duties and taxes shown both ways, score supplier and country risk, and choose a sourcing strategy. Capital projects: a cost plan from your bill, bid checks, a PERT schedule, a risk register, the investment case, earned value and stage gates. No email and no account. The formulas are the same ones in the ThreadForge Trade and Capital projects desks. Duties, freight and exchange rates are numbers you enter. This page does not look up a tariff, and it does not upload what you type.
