Live demo · ThreadForge 10.7.0

Sign up, then try the suite.

One free session, right in your browser. Enter an email first. A work address and a personal address are both accepted. Then run the workflows for an invented company, Example Co., including the TradeMath calculators.

  • An email opens the demo. Work or personal. This page keeps it in this browser. It is not sent to a server.
  • One session of 60 minutes. Each browser gets one. Ending it, or letting the timer run out, closes it.
  • Business workflows really work. Stock, orders, invoices, the ledger, CSV import, campaigns, roles and the trade calculators follow the product's rules.
  • AI, PC and data-source steps are simulated. They follow the product's documented behaviour on sample data and are labelled wherever they appear.

Use whichever inbox you actually read. A company domain is not required.

See pricing

Prefer to skip the signup? Open TradeMath. The same calculators, no email, on this site. Want another full session later? Email support@threadforgeofficial.com.

Ten things to try

  1. Receive a purchase order
  2. Fulfil a sale and open its invoice
  3. Record a payment in exact cents
  4. Preview and commit a CSV import
  5. Approve a campaign and see who consent leaves out
  6. Run a supplier due-diligence check
  7. Give the engineering agent a task and review its draft
  8. Plan an original app with the app builder
  9. Diagnose a PC and approve one repair
  10. Switch to the Viewer role and see what's read-only

A checklist follows you through the session, with a hint for each step. The TradeMath calculators are in the Trade section, separate from these ten.
