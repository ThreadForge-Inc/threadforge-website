Pioneer-class principle

Built to stand on its own, and to evolve with you.

The founding standard ThreadForge measures itself against. Version 0.9.0 candidate. Last updated 2026-10-26.

Why this page exists

Software that genuinely helps people has to outlive its own launch. This page records the commitments ThreadForge makes to the people who run their business, code, trade and machines on it, and the discipline that keeps those commitments honest. It is a standard, not a slogan: every claim named elsewhere on this site still needs the evidence and pricing support cited in the Evidence section and our product notices.

Seven commitments

  • Consumer sovereignty. The operator owns the data, the decisions and the outcomes. Delegation must be explicit.
  • Human approval for every commitment. Agents draft, route, audit and propose. They never merge, ship or commit without a human yes.
  • Protected contribution. Authorship and published work must stand on their own, not as a shadow or a derivative service. Records stay open for review and qualification.
  • No fabricated authority. Legal, licensing and pricing statements are made only where supported. Nothing on this page overrides the notice discipline in our pricing and release documents.
  • Safe, isolated cloud and IoT. Cloud, device and AI integrations are layered, isolated and reversible. They move with human intellect and machine intelligence together.
  • Continuous reconciliation. Where numbers or harmonised state matter, the model tolerates change. No single constant is treated as final truth.
  • Steady core, evolving edges. The core stands while new capability layers are added. New work strengthens it; it is never silently replaced.

How this shapes what ships

Each capability is published with evidence and a review trail. Cloud, device and AI integration is declared only where it is isolated, safe and reversible. Pricing and status text are drawn only from the owner-approved evidence list and product notices, with competitor prices re-checked before each update.

The numbers and statuses printed on this site match the latest recorded release evidence, and the roadmap and questions and answers are updated together when a launch gate closes.
