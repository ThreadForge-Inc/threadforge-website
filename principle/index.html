1. Purpose & Founding Mandate

Software that genuinely serves operators has to outlive its own launch. This page records the pioneering, local-first and cloud-isolated agentic standard that the ThreadForge suite upholds from single-desk operations to industrial-scale deployments. It is a living standard, not a slogan: every capability, release status and commercial tier published across this site is backed by deterministic test evidence, SHA-256 verification and the product notices cited in our Evidence and Pricing sections.

2. Seven Core Commitments (The “Class Pioneer”)

  • Consumer-first sovereignty. The suite serves the operator before it serves any platform. Ownership of data, SQLite ledgers, decisions and outcomes stays with the operator unless explicitly delegated.
  • Agentic intelligence with human oversight. AI agents draft, route, audit and propose; they never merge, ship or commit without an explicit human yes. The agent stack proposes; the human disposes.
  • Open, auditable, protected contribution. Authorship and published engineering work stand on their own as an original contribution, never as a shadow or derivative service. Processes, review trails, test counts and checksums stay open for qualification and verification.
  • No fabricated authority. Commercial, legal, licensing and pricing statements are made only where supported by verified builds and product notices. Nothing on this page overrides the notice discipline in our pricing and release documentation.
  • Safe, isolated cloud, mobile and desktop posture. Cloud, Android, Windows MSIX and local AI integrations (including local Ollama on 127.0.0.1:11434) are layered, isolated and reversible—moving with human intellect and machine intelligence together.
  • Continuous reconciliation & trigonometric accounting. Where landed cost, capital BOQ, HRBP workforce analytics or harmonised state matter, the deterministic model tolerates continuous evolution. Algorithms drive the protocol, and no single static constant is treated as final truth.
  • Steady core, evolving edges (Contribution without erasure). The authored core stands steadfast while new capability layers—such as the Moonwalk v17 HRBP Toolkit and Local Media Tools—strengthen it without silently replacing the foundation.
