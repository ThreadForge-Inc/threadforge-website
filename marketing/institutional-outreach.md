# ThreadForge institutional outreach kit

**Owner:** Threadforge Inc. · President & Founder Bilal Ahmed Khan
**Landing page:** https://threadforgeofficial.com/institutions/

**Last checked against the site:** 2026-10-30 (release 10.16.1 / Moonwalk v17)

The approach is the Mentor's: say what it does, show where to check, and say what is not done yet. Institutional buyers (universities, ministries, NGOs, corporate procurement) reject vendors who overclaim. They tend to trust vendors who list their own gaps first.

---

## 1. Rules for every message

**Do**
- Link to something checkable in every message: the demo, the Store listing, the release checksums, or the readiness table.
- Say "one-time price, unlimited organizations and seats" early. For institutions this is the most unusual fact.
- Mention the open items (code signing for the direct installer, terms in drafting, security and accessibility reviews planned) before they ask.
- Keep the first email under 150 words. Ask one question.

**Don't**
- Claim certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, WCAG conformance), awards, rankings or "best in class".
- Call a due-diligence report a sanctions clearance or legal advice.
- Promise discounts, custom features, SLAs or delivery dates that Threadforge Inc. has not decided.
- Send bulk or automated email. Write to named people, one at a time, and stop after two follow-ups.

For Pakistan, natural starting points include university departments, national and city chambers of commerce, trade-development and SME-support bodies, and software parks or incubators. Confirm the current contact person on each body's own website before writing.

## 6. Answers to the questions reviewers ask

| Question | Honest answer |
|---|---|
| Is it certified (SOC 2, ISO 27001)? | No. We don't hold these certifications. An independent security review with a penetration test is planned. |
| Is the installer signed? | The Microsoft Store build is distributed by the Store. Trusted code signing for the direct installer is in progress, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning for it until then. |
| Where is our data stored? | On your own PC or server. Threadforge Inc. does not host your records. |
| Does AI send our data to a third party? | Not if you use a local model through Ollama. If you connect your own provider, usage goes to that provider under your account and budget. |
| Can we audit the code? | Yes, with Industrial Park, which includes the source for your version. |
| Is it accessible (WCAG)? | An accessibility review by a qualified reviewer is planned and not done yet. We don't claim conformance. |
| Do you have licence and privacy terms? | They are being drafted with legal counsel. We can share the current draft on request. |
| Is supplier screening a sanctions clearance? | No. It is decision support with a source for every finding. Use your own counsel for sanctions decisions. |
| Is there an academic, non-profit or volume price? | Decided case by case by Threadforge Inc. Don't quote a figure until the owner approves it. |
