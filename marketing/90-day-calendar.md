# ThreadForge 90-day content calendar (2 November 2026 – 31 January 2027)

One plan for every channel, built only from what already exists: the posts in this folder, the three approved
films and their 9:16 reels, the Academy, the live demo and the Institutions page. Every post follows the brand
kit's voice: plain, exact and checkable, no superlatives, and nothing the website does not say.

## Rhythm every week

| Channel | How often | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn (company and founder) | 2 posts | Monday: the week's theme. Thursday: one proof or one lesson. |
| X | 3 posts | Short facts from `x-thread.md`, one film clip, one reply thread. |
| YouTube Shorts, Instagram and TikTok Reels | 1 reel | The 9:16 reels, unchanged, with the week's caption. |
| WhatsApp broadcast | every second week | `whatsapp.md`, updated to the week's theme. |
| Institutional email | 10 messages | From `institutional-outreach.md`, one segment per week. |
| Reddit | once a month | `reddit.md`, as a question, in a community that welcomes it. |

Every post ends with one link: the live demo, a film, the Academy, or the Institutions page. Never two.

### Dates that depend on something else

- **Google Play live:** use week 12 for it, and only after the listing is published. Until then, say "Android
  APK"; the site checker refuses wording that calls a Play listing live.
- **Microsoft Store 10.17.2 certified:** one LinkedIn and one X post that week, about the Store edition and the
  early-buyer courtesy.
- **Urdu, Arabic and Chinese pages:** when they are published, repeat weeks 1 to 3 in each language.
- **Signed Windows installer:** when it is signed, say so in one post and remove the SmartScreen caveat everywhere.

## What to measure every Friday

- Live demo sessions started.
- Trial starts and paid licences, and how many trials turned into licences.
- Microsoft Store installs.
- Academy visits and handout downloads.
- Institutional conversations opened, and their next step.

Put more effort into the step that loses the most people. Do not judge a channel on likes alone.

## Before anything is posted

- Is every fact on the website today? If not, leave it out.
- Is there exactly one link, and does it work?
- Does a price say "once"?
- No "best", "#1", "certified", "enterprise-ready", or "on Google Play" before it is true.
