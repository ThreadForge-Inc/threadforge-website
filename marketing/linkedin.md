# LinkedIn — ThreadForge 10.16.1

We just shipped ThreadForge 10.16.1.

ThreadForge is a private, local-first suite for trade sourcing, supplier due diligence, capital projects and PC care. This release lands three things a small business can feel from day one:

1. A clear licensing model — Essentials, Professional, Industrial, Industrial Park. No licence means you still get a working Essentials desk; your existing records are always readable.
2. A new brand film and a lighter, faster release pipeline (leaner CI, verified Windows builds, MSIX for the Microsoft Store).
3. Improved read-only diagnostics that never change your machine without consent.

We’re submitting the signed MSIX to the Microsoft Store this week. In the meantime, Windows installers and docs are at https://threadforgeofficial.com.

If you want a named licence or want to pilot it with your team, reach out: support@threadforgeofficial.com
