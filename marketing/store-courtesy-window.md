## LinkedIn

A small, honest detail about how ThreadForge's Microsoft Store edition is licensed.

Since version 10.17.0, the Store edition sells as Essentials ($49). Before that it sold as Professional.
The Store doesn't tell us who bought the app, so there's no way to send a licence file to Store buyers
individually. Instead, ThreadForge checks something it already has: the date your workspace was created,
recorded once when it's made and never changed afterward.

A Store workspace created before 1 November 2026 (00:00 UTC) keeps Professional, including PC care
repairs, at no extra cost. One created from that date on gets Essentials, as currently listed. It's not a
promotion — just how the transition from the old pricing to the new pricing is handled fairly for people
who already had the app.

Full pricing: https://threadforgeofficial.com/#pricing
