What's included

One desk. The records a small business actually keeps.

What this does

  • Stock and products, with a validated movement ledger and an auditable reason recorded for every change.
  • Sales and purchase orders as drafts, which don't reserve stock until they're fulfilled or received.
  • Customers and suppliers (contacts), kept in your own workspace.
  • Internal payment records and an exact-cent rate & policy log.
  • Campaign briefs with an owner review step, before anything goes out.
  • Role segregation: administrator, operator, finance and viewer roles, each limited to what their role needs.

What this doesn't do

  • Move money, process a card payment, or connect to a bank. This is a bookkeeping record, not a payment processor or a digital wallet.
  • Issue a tax invoice or give tax or legal advice. Verify tax and legal requirements yourself.
  • Store a card number or any payment credential, anywhere.
  • Send email or chat notifications on your behalf.
